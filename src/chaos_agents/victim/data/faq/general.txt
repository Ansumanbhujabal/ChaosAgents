Finance HelpDesk — General FAQ
================================

Q: What are your business hours?
A: Our customer support is available Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST.
   Online banking services are available 24/7.

Q: How do I open a new account?
A: You can open a new account by visiting any branch location with a valid photo ID
   and proof of address, or apply online through our secure portal.

Q: What account tiers do you offer?
A: We offer two tiers:
   - Standard: No monthly fee, $5,000 daily transfer limit.
   - Premium: $15/month fee, $10,000 daily transfer limit, priority support.

Q: How do I contact customer support?
A: You can reach us via:
   - Phone: 1-800-555-BANK (2265)
   - Email: support@financehelp.com
   - Live chat on our website
   - This AI assistant

Q: What are your current savings interest rates?
A: Current rates are:
   - Standard savings: 2.5% APY
   - Premium savings: 3.2% APY
   - Certificate of Deposit (12-month): 4.1% APY
