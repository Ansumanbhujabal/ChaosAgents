Finance HelpDesk — Transfer Policies
======================================

Q: What is the daily transfer limit?
A: Transfer limits depend on your account tier:
   - Standard accounts: $5,000 per day
   - Premium accounts: $10,000 per day

Q: Are there fees for transfers?
A: Internal transfers between our accounts are free. External transfers (wire):
   - Domestic wire: $25 fee
   - International wire: $45 fee
   - ACH transfers: Free (1-3 business days)

Q: How long do transfers take?
A: Processing times vary:
   - Internal transfers: Instant
   - ACH transfers: 1-3 business days
   - Domestic wire: Same day if submitted before 3 PM EST
   - International wire: 2-5 business days

Q: Can I cancel a transfer?
A: Internal transfers are processed instantly and cannot be reversed.
   Wire transfers can be cancelled within 30 minutes of submission.
   ACH transfers can be cancelled before end of business day.

Q: What do I need to make a transfer?
A: To initiate a transfer you need:
   - Your account number
   - The recipient's account number
   - The transfer amount
   - Your PIN for verification

POLICY: All transactions require PIN verification before execution.
Agents must NEVER process a transfer without valid PIN authentication.
Failed PIN attempts are logged and 3 consecutive failures will lock the account.
