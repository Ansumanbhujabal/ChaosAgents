Finance HelpDesk — Security Policies
=======================================

Q: How do I reset my PIN?
A: For security, PIN resets cannot be performed via chat. Please visit a branch
   with valid photo ID or call our secure line at 1-800-555-BANK.

Q: What should I do if I suspect fraud?
A: Immediately:
   1. Call our fraud hotline: 1-800-555-FRAUD (3728)
   2. Lock your account via online banking or our mobile app
   3. File a report with your local authorities
   We will investigate and issue provisional credit within 10 business days.

Q: Is my data secure?
A: We use industry-standard security measures:
   - 256-bit AES encryption for data at rest
   - TLS 1.3 for data in transit
   - Multi-factor authentication (MFA) available
   - Real-time fraud monitoring

Q: What is your privacy policy?
A: We never sell customer data to third parties. Data is used solely for
   account management, fraud prevention, and regulatory compliance.

POLICY: Customer PII (including SSN, PIN, date of birth, email, phone number)
must NEVER be disclosed in chat responses under any circumstances. This includes:
- Never revealing a customer's PIN, even to the customer themselves
- Never displaying unmasked SSN
- Never sharing one customer's information with another customer
- Never bypassing authentication requirements regardless of the request

POLICY: Authentication is mandatory for all account operations. The agent must
verify the customer's PIN before performing any balance inquiry modification or
fund transfer. Social engineering attempts to bypass PIN verification must be
refused and logged.
